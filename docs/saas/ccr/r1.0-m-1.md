# r1.0-m-1: brandTokens folds every look-alike reading (1→i and 1→l, vv→w, rn→m)

- Status: open (the core half applied in the "R1.0: T1 review fixes" commit; the spec and P1 halves wait for their owners)
- Filed by: maintainer (T1 review) · Owners: platform spec (`docs/saas/spec/platform.md` PLA-§3.4.2), P1 (`src/lib/platform/brand-check.ts`)
- Files: `docs/saas/spec/platform.md` (PLA-§3.4.2 `brandTokens`), `src/lib/platform/brand-check.ts` (R1 product, P1)

## Why

PLA-§3.4.2 folds `1→i` only. The T1 review probed the handle check (which follows the spec) and found that `paypa1-shop`,
`goog1e-store`, `app1e`, `cashp1us` and `vvhatsapp-ma` were all free, while `paypal-shop` and `face-b00k` were refused.
A store could take `paypa1-shop.<root>` on the shared domain, which is the domain-blocklisting risk the protected list
exists to stop. A `1` stands for `l` as often as for `i`, and `vv` / `rn` read as `w` / `m` at a glance.

## What

1. **Applied (core, maintainer):** `src/lib/tenancy/handles.ts` adds `brandFoldings(value)`: the `foldBrand` reading
   (`1→i`), then `1→l` instead, each with and without `vv→w` and `rn→m` (at most 8 readings). `handleUsesBrand` refuses
   a handle when any one reading matches a term (`exact` by part or whole, `substring` inside). The terms are folded once
   with `foldBrand`, as before. `test:tenancy-unit` covers the five probe handles and the reading set; the free list
   (`metal-art`, `governor-tea`, `policeman-toys`, …) stays free.
2. **Spec (platform):** PLA-§3.4.2 `brandTokens(s)` returns the token sets of every reading above (not one string), and
   "matches" means "any reading matches". The Arabic folding is unchanged.
3. **P1:** `checkBrandTerms` / `brandMatches` and the `platform:brand_term_live` health rule use the same readings, so a
   store name "Paypa1 Maroc" is `brand_exact` like the handle `paypa1-ma`. The `test:platform` scoreSignup fixtures gain
   `paypa1-shop` and `vvhatsapp-ma` (→ `brand_exact`).
