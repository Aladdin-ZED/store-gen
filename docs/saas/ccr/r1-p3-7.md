## What

Core §H.2 slot 8 lists "leads > 30 days" in `housekeeping`, and landing.md LAN-§10.3 / §10.6 call 30 days the
retention window of the inbox ("Leads older than 30 days are purged by the housekeeping job per store"). In my base,
`src/lib/jobs/tracks/core.ts` is still empty (P8 has not merged), so the row cannot be checked yet. Two asks:

1. P8's `housekeeping` keeps the row: per store (`forEachStore`, states every state but `purged`), call the purge with
   30 days, within the slot's 4 s.
2. The purge deletes every lead older than 30 days, converted or not. Today's `purgeAbandonedLeads` keeps a converted
   lead forever (`isNull(leads.convertedOrderId)`), but from R1 a lead carries more than a phone (`sel`, `note`,
   `lost_reason`, attempts): its order already holds what the store keeps, and the inbox never lists a lead past 30
   days, so a converted lead older than the window is dead data with a phone number in it.

```diff
 /** Housekeeping of one store: its abandoned leads older than `days` (the cron's per-store loop, ORD-13). */
 export async function purgeAbandonedLeads(scope: WriteScope, days = 30): Promise<number> {
   const res = await db
     .delete(leads)
-    .where(whereStore(leads, scope, isNull(leads.convertedOrderId), lte(leads.createdAt, new Date(Date.now() - days * 86_400_000))));
+    .where(whereStore(leads, scope, lte(leads.createdAt, new Date(Date.now() - days * 86_400_000))));
   return affected(res);
 }
```

## Why

LAN-§10.6 acceptance bullet 5 and LP-06's "leads > 30 days are purged". Lead attribution (LD12) and the 30-day header
(`leadCounts`) only read the last 30 days, so nothing of P3 reads an older row.

## Effect

The leads table stays bounded per store; the converted rows past 30 days go too. `test:leads` (case "purge") keeps
passing on either form (it checks unconverted rows, per store); the maintainer adds a converted-row case when this
lands.
