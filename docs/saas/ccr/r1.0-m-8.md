## What

1. **Sheets ownership proof (SHT-09, OPE-§19.3 steps 3-4).** The code goes in the spreadsheet's name or a tab's name,
   and verify reads the titles only (one metadata call); no cell is ever read. The rows of an orders tab carry what
   shoppers type (address 240 chars, note 300), so store B could plant the code in store A's first rows by ordering
   from A's page, then connect A's sheet: read its title, rewrite its header, append B's orders to it. Connect and
   verify are secret writes under `integrations.manage`: a fresh step-up (§B.7), `meta.stepUp` on
   `integrations.sheets.connected`. P5's `sheet_bindings` keeps the same flow with the binding insert.
2. **`pending_sheets` (§H.2 slot 1, SHT-11).** The predicate now has the spec's "or a pull due" branch: Sheets enabled,
   `pull` not off, and the store's `s:<sid>:sheets:pull` bucket outside its window; such stores come after every store
   with orders to push. A store whose orders were all synced had its sheet statuses pulled only by a manual sync.
3. **Agents (§A.3.2 `agent*`), R1.0 interim.** No assignment exists before P4, so an agent works the store's whole order
   and lead list (the single-tenant `agent` role's behaviour), without `orders.read_all`, `orders.export`,
   `blocklist.write` or any page, settings or integrations key. P4 adds the `assignee` predicate to the order and lead
   repositories for `OWN_QUEUE_KEYS` (`ownQueueOnly`). test:tenancy models it (the T4 review cases).
4. **Media disk cache (MED-01, MED-07, §E.6).** A large file's disk copy is named `<storeId>.<sha256(media key)>` in
   `tmpdir()/store-gen-media`: never by content, so two stores' identical bytes never share a copy (no cross-store
   timing, no cross-store eviction), and no longer COD Studio's directory. `evictStore(storeId)` drops a store's
   memory and disk entries; P8's purge job calls it, P8's `MEDIA_CACHE_DIR` / `DATA_DIR` replace the directory.
5. **Visitor-IP diagnostic.** The old Settings → Security check (`requestNetworkView`, TRUSTED_PROXY_HOPS) is on the
   console home behind `health.read` (English, the console's R1 language); P1's `/admin/health` takes it over.
6. **The store shell (P2).** The `[store]` layout renders the dashboard in English, left to right (`lang="en"
   dir="ltr"`, the English catalog) until its catalogs exist (§I.5, D36, R3 UX-08); the translated team page sets the
   viewer's language and direction itself. The layout also renders the step-up sheet once (`StepUpProvider`,
   `useStoreStepUp()`): every refused `step_up` (an action result or a route's 403) asks for the proof and retries
   once. The Orders CSV is fetched (not a plain download link) so its §B.7 refusal opens that sheet.
7. **Screen keys (§I.1).** `/integrations/pixels` = `pixels.manage`, `/integrations/sheets` = `integrations.manage`,
   `/exports` = `export.create`, `/generate` = `pages.write` + `ai.use`; the nav items follow. A call agent's nav has no
   Overview (it is always redirected to Orders).

## Why

The T4 review (R1.0 step 5): items 1 and 4 are cross-store paths of the MED-01 / SHT-09 classes, item 2 a regression of
SHT-11, items 3, 5 and 7 decisions the code made implicitly, item 6 two working flows the move broke (an `ar` owner's
mirrored English shell, the Orders CSV refused by its new step-up).

## Effect on others

P5: OPE-§19.3 amended (titles, step-up); P4: the predicate hook is theirs; P8: the directory and `evictStore`; P1: move
the diagnostic into `/admin/health`; P2: keep `StepUpProvider` and the English/LTR wrapper until the catalogs land.
