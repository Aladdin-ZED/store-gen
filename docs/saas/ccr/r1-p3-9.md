
and in the `//` note: "assist-ty 1500 -> 3600 (CCR r1-p3-9, measured 2026-11-03: 3434 B): the thank-you `{when}` bundles
operations' `nextCallableAt` (≈ 1,040 B gz alone), not the ≈ 300 B LAN-§5.2 estimated".

## Why

LAN-§8.3 step 3 and the rev 4 CRIT3 rule make the thank-you's `{when}` come from **the same function the queue uses**
(`nextCallableAt`, src/lib/callcenter/window.ts, P4), bundled into `assist-ty`, so the page and the call ladder agree by
construction. LAN-§5.2 budgeted the chunk at 1,500 B on the estimate "≈ 300 B for `nextCallableAt` and `reviveWindow`
together". Measured with the asset build's own esbuild options (es2019, minified, gzip -9):

| Part of `assist-ty` | B minified |
|---|---|
| `nextCallableAt` and its scan (operations' window.ts, frozen) | 1,941 (≈ 1,040 B gz alone) |
| `reviveWindow`, `callWhen`, `wallTime` (src/lib/landing/hours.ts) | ≈ 1,100 |
| `confirmText` (operations' template grammar), `firstWord`, `cutAtWord` | ≈ 600 |
| `formatMoney` (the runtime's money format, for `{total}`) | ≈ 600 |
| `localParts`, `addDays` (src/lib/delivery/eta.ts) | ≈ 490 |
| storage names, the chunk (order record, pill, beacon, sentence, link) | ≈ 2,100 |
| **total** | **≈ 7,000 B minified, 3,434 B gz** |

Every part is already as small as I could make it without forking operations' function (the date helpers share one
parser, the template filler is one pass, the revival checks only what the scan does not). The only way under 1,500 B
would be a second, browser-only calling-window evaluator, which is exactly what CRIT3 forbids. 3,600 B leaves the
5 % tolerance for P4's merged window.ts.

The `thanks-page` cap needs no change: render-check measures `thanks` + `pixels` + `assist-ty` together at 12,839 B,
inside 12,600 B + 5 % (13,230 B). The margin is thin; when R3 adds `chain`, re-measure the cap then (LAN-§5.2 already
reserves 14,600 B for it). A store whose Purchase trigger is not `order` ships no `pixels` chunk on its thank-you
(`purchaseInBrowser = false`), so most COD thank-you pages stay near 10 KB.

`assist` measures 2,694 B gz against its 2,600 B budget (inside render-check's 5 %), and the heaviest product page
stays inside `page` (render-check: 34,274 B, cap 34,800): no change requested there.

## Effect

Only the one number and the note change; no chunk, no document and no golden changes. The DB F5 page weight is
unaffected in practice (the thank-you page is the light one).
