### 5. §J.1 `test:ownership` row: record the resolution order the check applies

Proposed sentence after "fails on any file matched by two tracks of the same phase":

> A path's owner is the strongest claim:
> 1. a track's named path (no `**`, no `*` in the file name);
> 2. the maintainer's named path;
> 3. a track's glob;
> 4. the maintainer's glob.
>
> Two tracks at the strongest level is a finding. So a file a track names inside another track's glob (P1's
> `nav/console.ts` in P2's `shell/**`) is not an overlap, while two globs that intersect are. The §K.3 list applies in
> every phase at the maintainer's level, so in R1.0 T3's `src/lib/edge/**` and T1's `settings/index.ts` win over it.
> C0a's rows own nothing in R1.0, because C0a merged at the phase start.
>
> Overlaps are looked for on the working tree and on one witness path per pattern of every phase. The current phase
> (the manifest's) fails the run. Later phases print warnings until their contract step writes their manifest, or
> until `--phase <P>` names them.

The header of `scripts/qa/ownership-check.ts` lists the reading rules: braces, `except` / `outside`, bare names,
the `nav` / `i18n` / `jobs` / `commands` shorthands, "`x` is P4's", and commentary in parentheses.
