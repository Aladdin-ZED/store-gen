## Why

- §H.13 and §J.1 (rev 3, ENGP-12): render-check "measures every add-on chunk and the page caps from
  `budgets.json.compositions` (`page` 34,800 B, `thanks-page` 12,600 B)", so the `heaviest` list goes away.
- Without this change, `thanks-page` is not measured at all.
- When `assist` is built, `page` keeps measuring the old list without it, and the 34,800 B cap no longer guards the
  real heaviest page.

- It adds one line, `thanks-page`: 10,003 B of the 12,600 B cap (`thanks` and `pixels` gzipped together, measured
  2026-10-24 on base `5bbef59`).

  R1.0 item and is not part of this CCR.

  `assist-ty` or `track`.
