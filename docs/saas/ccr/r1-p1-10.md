# r1-p1-10: what the approval queue and the stores list need from P2

- Status: open (P1 codes against the current files meanwhile; nothing here blocks the step)

## 1. `HealthRing` has no 24 px size (P2, `src/components/admin/common/health-ring.tsx`)

### Why

PLA-§6.1 draws the stores list's Health column as a "24 px ring with the score … the tooltip lists components", and
PLA-§3.5 the queue row's "score ring (24 px, stroke tinted by band)". The frozen `HealthRingProps.size` is
`40 | 56 | 96` (PLA-§22.12), so neither can use the primitive at that size, and the queue's ring is a risk score with
band tints, not a health score.

### What

Add `24` to `HealthRingProps.size` (`size: 24 | 40 | 56 | 96`) in the P2 body and in
`src/lib/contracts/r1-p0b-platform.ts`. Until then the console draws both 24 px rings with its own
`src/components/console/ring.tsx` (`ScoreRing`, tinted by `src/components/console/tints.ts`: health ≥ 80 emerald,
50-79 neutral, 30-49 yellow, < 30 crimson, the same bands as PLA-§6.1 and the primitive's doc comment). The verify
step swaps the stores list's ring to `<HealthRing size={24} surface="console" … />` once the size exists; the queue
keeps `ScoreRing` (a risk score is not a health score).

## 2. `StatusPill` and `Monogram` stubs render ids and a fixed size (P2)

### Why

`StatusPill`'s P0b stub prints the raw status id (`pending`, `scheduled_deletion`); `Monogram`'s stub ignores `size`.
The queue detail, the linked accounts and the stores table use both with their frozen props.

### What

Nothing beyond P2's planned bodies: the label of each status (the console copy is already in
`src/lib/i18n/admin/en/console.ts` as `console.status.<status>`, if P2 wants the same words: In review, Active,
Restricted, Suspended, Closed, Scheduled for deletion, Purged, Not approved) with its icon, and the 24 / 32 / 48 px
monogram. Until the merge, `src/components/console/shell/console.module.css` lays the stubs out with `:not([class])`
rules (a 32 px monogram rule was added in this step); they stop matching once P2's bodies carry classes.

## 3. `StepUpSheet` is still the stub (P2)

`Reject + Also ban this account` answers `{ ok: false, error: "step_up" }` (queue.ts, `users.manage` ↑); the split
view opens `<StepUpSheet surface="console" open onVerified onClose />` and re-sends the decision with the same
operation id on `onVerified`. With the stub the sheet renders an empty dialog, so the ban path is only usable once
P2's body (the TOTP form calling `stepUpAction`) lands. No prop change is needed.
