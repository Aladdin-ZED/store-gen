# r1-p1-1: the signup path uses P1's scorer helpers, and two copies become one export

- Status: open (P1 codes against the current files meanwhile: two pinned copies, and T2's own auto-approval rule)
- Filed by: P1 CONSOLE (R1 step 1, `p1-core-pure`) · Owners of the files: maintainer for the R1 phase (T2's
  `src/lib/auth/**` and the read-only `src/lib/orders/risk.ts` have no R1 owner), T4's `src/lib/admin-data/security.ts`
  (no R1 owner either; `ownership-check --owner` answers "read-only in this phase" for all four)
- Files: `src/lib/auth/signup.ts`, `src/lib/auth/identity.ts`, `src/lib/orders/risk.ts`, `src/lib/admin-data/security.ts`

## Why

P1 replaced C0a's `scoreSignup` stub (`src/lib/platform/signup-risk.ts`, PLA-§3.4). Four places outside P1's files
still hold their own copy of something the scorer now owns, or ignore what it now answers:

1. **T2's auto-approval and hold class.** `src/lib/auth/signup.ts` declares its own `HOLD_CLASS` (line 198) and its
   own `autoApproval()` (line 248), which equal PLA-§3.4.1 today. P1 now exports both (`HOLD_CLASS`, `autoApprove`
   from `src/lib/platform/signup-risk.ts`, pure bodies in `signup-band.ts`), and `test:platform` proves `autoApprove`
   against the whole truth table (640 rows: mode × band × hold × human check × trusted × setting). Two copies of one rule drift.
2. **The trusted creator (PD31) never applies.** `createAdditionalStore` passes `trustedCreator: false` to
   `autoApproval` (line 537, "P1's trustedCreator() signal is not in R1.0"). P1's scorer now checks PD31 for `/app/new`
   (`via: "app_new"` with `existingUserId`) and answers it as the `trusted_creator` flag (−30). Without the change below
   a trusted creator still lands in the queue in `approval` mode, which PD31 says it must not.
3. **`describeUserAgent`** (`src/lib/admin-data/security.ts:28`) gives the `signals.inputs.uaLabel` and the `bot_ua` /
   `no_ua` evidence, but its module loads `next/headers`, the session, the audit log and the database client. The
   scorer keeps a copy (`signup-risk.ts`), and `test:platform` pins the copy to the original line by line.
4. **`BOT_UA`** (`src/lib/orders/risk.ts:38`) is not exported. The scorer keeps a copy, pinned the same way.
5. **`normalizeEmailForIdentity`** exists twice: T2's `src/lib/auth/identity.ts` (which writes the `email_norm` link)
   and P1's `src/lib/platform/normalize.ts` (PLA-§3.4.2 names it as P1's). They are the same algorithm today; the
   links the scorer looks up must hash exactly what signup writes.

2. `src/lib/admin-data/security.ts` and `src/lib/orders/risk.ts`: move `describeUserAgent` and the `BOT_UA` pattern to

3. `src/lib/auth/identity.ts`: `export { normalizeEmailForIdentity } from "@/lib/platform/normalize";` in place of its
   own body (P1's file is pure and isomorphic, so nothing new loads). Until then P1's DB harness (`test:platform-db`,
   step 2) pins the links `identityLinksFor()` computes to the rows `signupIdentityLinks()` writes.

## Proof after the change

- `test:platform`: the 56 golden fixtures, the `autoApprove` truth table and the pins (the pins are removed with item 2).
- T2's `selftest-identity` (`/app/new` cases): a creator who owns an `active` store older than 30 days with no
  restriction in 90 days gets an `active` store in `approval` mode with `reason_code = "auto_trusted"`; a creator whose
  store was restricted 10 days ago gets a `pending` one.
