## Why

- The case seeds `ops.new_orders` as "a kind no track registered yet" and asserts it reads as group `store`. Since P4
  step 2, `src/lib/notify/kinds/ops.ts` registers `ops.new_orders` (OPE-§21.4: store_role owner/admin/manager, batched
  5 min, vars `{ count, sinceTime }`, link `/orders?view=to-call`) in group `orders`, the "Orders" filter of the
  notification centre (PLA-§22, "filters by group: Orders · Team · Store · …"). The fixture's premise no longer holds,
  so the check fails in P4's worktree: `{"store":[7,10],"orders":[8]}`, 6,596 passed, 1 failed.
- The check itself (the fallback group of an unregistered kind) stays right; only its sample kind has to be one that
  no track will ever register. The optional second hunk keeps a positive control for a registered kind.
