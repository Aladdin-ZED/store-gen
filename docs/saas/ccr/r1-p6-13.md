# CCR r1-p6-13: builder mounts and the G28 brand lock (step 12)

**From:** P6 DESIGN, step 12 [builder-design]. **To:** the maintainer at the P3w1, P7 and P6 merges (and P3 / P7 for the props).

## What

Step 12 ships the Design check panel, the DNA card, Differentiate, Bolder / Calmer / Upgrade and "Apply my brand".
Three components and one action it should call do not exist in the P6 base, because P3w1 and P7 merge before P6 and
are not in this worktree. Each mount below is a few lines in a P6-owned file.

| What | Where (P6 file) | Today |
|---|---|---|
| P3's `<ProofAttestSheet>` at the `sample` toggle of proof sections and at the rating field | `src/components/builder/fields/Fields.tsx` (the `sample` toggle and the rating field renderers) | not mounted |
| The panel's honesty rows deep-link to `ProofAttestSheet` | `src/components/builder/panels/DesignCheckPanel.tsx`, `goto()` for `item.source === "honesty"` | selects the row's section (the canvas scrolls to it and outlines it), where the sheet will open |
| P7's `<FieldRewrite>` on text fields | `src/components/builder/fields/Fields.tsx` (text and textarea renderers) | not mounted |
| P7's voice action for the G28 brand lock ("Lock for my brand" on Voice) | `src/lib/design/builder.server.ts`, `writeBrandLock`, the `G28` branch | writes `store_ai.voice.register` through the core's `updateStoreSetting` (audited `store.settings.updated`) |

## Why

The brief (p6-12) asks P6 to mount them (DES-§15.5). Importing modules that do not exist would break tsc in the P6
worktree, so step 12 codes against what exists and keeps one seam per mount.

## Exact change at the merge

1. `Fields.tsx`: import `ProofAttestSheet` from P3's module; render it beside the `sample` toggle of the proof sections
   (`testimonials`, `socialProof`, `stats`, `reviews`) and beside the rating field, with the section id and field path.
2. `DesignCheckPanel.tsx` `goto()`: for `item.source === "honesty"`, open the sheet for `item.section` instead of only
   selecting the section (the selection stays as the fallback when the sheet cannot open).
3. `Fields.tsx`: wrap the text / textarea renderers' input in P7's `<FieldRewrite path={…} value={…} onApply={…}>`
   (applies through `setSectionField`, one undo step).
4. `builder.server.ts` `writeBrandLock`: replace the `G28` branch's `updateStoreSetting(scope, "store_ai", …)` with P7's
   voice action (same `register` value, same `brand.write` gate on the P6 action that calls it).

## Not changed

No shared file is edited. The builder's left tabs (`LeftTab` in `src/lib/editor/store.ts`) are untouched: the Design
