## What

Case (e) gains one assertion: the purge's plan covers the tenant set exactly. `purgePlan()` lives in
`src/lib/jobs/purge/plan.ts` (pure: schema.ts and the DATA_AREAS registry, no database, no `server-only`), not in
`src/lib/jobs/purge.ts` as this CCR first said; `src/lib/jobs/purge.ts` is the job that runs it.

- `purgePlan().tables`: the tenant tables deleted `WHERE store_id = ?` (each once).
- `purgePlan().keep`: the tenant tables a purge keeps (`PURGE_KEEP` ∩ the schema: audit_log, impersonation_sessions,
  platform_requests, abuse_reports, legal_acceptances, signup_reviews, platform_notes, store_handle_history).
- The assertion: `tables ∪ keep` = every `TABLE_LEVELS` row of level `T`, `P*` or `P-opt` (the §D.2 / §D.3 rows with
  a `store_id`), with no table in both and none twice. `tenantSetProblems()` (already in case e) ties `TABLE_LEVELS` to
  schema.ts, so the chain is document = TABLE_LEVELS = schema.ts = the purge.
