## What

### 1. F15 compares coverage relative to each script's regular text (DES-§10.2 F15, DES-§10.5 "Arabic display weight")

Replace "the rendered ink coverage of the Latin display word … and of the Arabic display word … is within 0.85-1.15
(ink area / bbox area …)" with:

> the ink coverage (ink area / ink bbox area, from the flattened outlines) of the Arabic display word "متجر الأمل" at
> `displayWeightAr`, **divided by the median coverage of the same word in the script's shipped body faces at 400**, is
> within 0.85-1.15 of the same quotient for the Latin display word "Hamburgefonstiv" at `w` (`scripts/qa/design-ink.ts`).

Why: at equal weight the Arabic word's raw coverage is ≈ 0.83 × the Latin word's (its ink spreads over alef and lam
ascenders, deep bowls and a word space). Measured on the shipped files, the raw ratio put 57 of the 88 loaded pairings
outside the band, and every poster pairing (Latin 800-900: Archivo, Big Shoulders, Bricolage, Schibsted) out of reach
of any Arabic weight the faces ship (Arabic coverage tops out near 0.30 against 0.35-0.41). Relative to regular text,
"as dark as the Latin" is what the band can measure. With the calibration, every loaded pairing is in 0.85-1.15 after
the re-tunes below (references today: Latin 0.217, Arabic 0.179).

### 2. The catalogue values that differ from the DES-§10.3 table (re-tuned by F15, re-pointed by F4 / F11 / F14)

The light display serifs (Bodoni Moda hairlines, Instrument Serif, Fraunces 300-500) measure lighter than El Messiri
at its lightest weight (400), so El Messiri stays with Gloock and Young Serif and the light serifs take a light
geometric or kufi Arabic. The spec's own guidance ("Didone pairings prefer a lower-weight Arabic with some contrast,
El Messiri 400-500") fails F15 on the shipped outlines (ratio 1.57-1.78).

### 3. The look tokens (DES-§4.5.3, DESIGN.md §2.4)

Add the mask geometry tokens to the look token block: `--lp-mask-cut` (`calc(var(--lp-radius-card) × 2)` on `crisp`,
× 3 otherwise) and `--lp-mask-blob` (the seeded 6-point spline as a percentage `polygon()`, emitted only with
`look.mask = blob`). `--lp-line` is overridden by the look only where the depth recipe draws a hairline (flat and
non-light ambient keep the palette's line: removing it page-wide would erase list and form separators).

### 4. G3 margins (DES-§4.2 G3)

`wide` / `offset` gutters are fluid 20 → 64 px between 360 and 1280 px viewports (`narrow` 16 → 24): a 32 px phone
gutter leaves 326 px of a 390 px screen. The gene range is unchanged.

## Why

Keep the spec, the bible and the engine one text (DES-§3.6 is checked by `theme:check`; the rest is not).

## Effect on others

Docs only. No code of another track reads these values.
