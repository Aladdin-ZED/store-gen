## What

T4 moved the single-tenant panel to `/app/<handle>/**` (§I.1) and deleted `src/app/admin/(panel)/**`,
`(editor)/**`, `preview/**` and `src/app/api/admin/{export,sheets}/**`. The paths that still point into them:

1. `src/lib/auth/paths.ts`
   - `ADMIN_HOME_PATH = "/admin"`: no page answers it any more until `src/app/admin/page.tsx` (T2; the console
     overview from P1) exists. Post-login destinations come from `landingPathFor(memberships, lastStoreId,
     platformRole)` (§B.4), never `/admin`.
   - `SECURITY_SETTINGS_PATH = "/admin/settings/security"`: that page is gone. The two-step screen's "back" target
     becomes the account's security page (`/app/account…`, T2).
2. `homePathFor(role)` (legacy, `permissions.ts`): its `/admin…` targets are gone; its last T4 caller was removed (the
   dashboard now redirects a call agent to `/app/<h>/orders` itself).
3. `/app/<h>/team` (T2): the members screen. The deleted `src/app/admin/(panel)/settings/actions.ts` held the
   single-tenant team actions (`inviteUserAction`, `setUserRoleAction`, `setUserDisabledAction`,
   `resetUserPasswordAction`, `resetUserTwoFactorAction`) and the account actions (`changePasswordAction`,
   `revokeSessionAction`, `revokeOtherSessionsAction`); `TeamPanel` and `SecurityPanel` (T2's components) are
   unused until T2's pages render them with T2's store-scoped / user actions (`createInvite`, `userAction`, …).
4. `src/lib/auth/alias-route.ts` (`adminAliasRedirect`) has no caller left; the store aliases use
   `storeAliasRedirect` (src/lib/admin-data/shell.ts, T4).
5. The shell links the account at `ACCOUNT_PATH = "/app/account"` (src/components/admin/shell/app-sidebar.tsx) and the
   dashboard checklist's two-step item there too. If T2's account pages live elsewhere, T4 changes the constant
   (one line; tell T4).

## Why

§B.4, §I.1. Without (1) a sign-in lands on a 404 (`/admin`) and the two-step screen sends back to a deleted page.
