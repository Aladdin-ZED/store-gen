# r1-p8-17: how the runner gives budgets, for the tracks that register jobs (P1, P3, P4, P5, P6, P7)

- Status: **open, information only** (P8 step 3). No file of another track changes: each track picks its numbers in
  its own `src/lib/jobs/tracks/<track>.ts`. The maintainer reads it at each merge that registers jobs.
- Filed by: P8 · Owner of the files: each track (its tracks file) · Plan item: P8 step 3
- Files: `src/lib/jobs/tracks/{platform,ai,integrations,ops,landing,design}.ts` (no change requested)

## What

The runner (`src/lib/jobs/runner.ts`, `src/lib/jobs/slots.ts`) is real from P8 step 3. Its budget rule:

1. **A slot's budget is the guarantee and a job's `budgetMs` is its cap inside the slot.** A job starts with
   `ctx.deadline = now + min(budgetMs, what is left of its slot, what is left of the tick − 2 s)`. Jobs that share a
   slot run in registry order (integrations, core, ai, ops, landing, design, platform) and spend one allowance.
2. **Leftover goes only to slots 4c and 9.** Time a slot does not use is carried on. Slot 4c may add up to 5 s of it,
   slot 9 up to 6 s (slot 9 also holds the 5 s the table leaves unassigned). A job there gets the leftover only when
   its own `budgetMs` asks for it:
   - **P7 `ai.jobs`:** `budgetMs: 1_000` gets exactly 1 s; `budgetMs: 6_000` gets 1 s + up to 5 s of leftover.
   - **P1 `platform.health`:** `budgetMs: 3_000` gets exactly 3 s; up to `9_000` gets 3 s + up to 6 s of leftover.
     `platform.alarms` runs in what remains of slot 9 after the jobs before it in `platform.ts`. Register it **first**
     (≤ 1 s), or give `platform.health` a cap below 9 s, so a budget-driven health run never leaves alarms 0 ms.
3. `test:jobs` refuses a `budgetMs` above slot + leftover cap (`jobBudgetProblems`): 13 s in slot 3, 6 s in 4c,
   9 s in 9, the slot's own budget elsewhere.
4. Slots by name (`slotOf`): every R1 job name of §H.2, OPE-§J, AI-§N, DES-§24.1, LAN-§23.4 and PLA-§15 is in the
   table. A new name falls back by `every`: tick → 7, hourly or daily → 6; `platform.*` → 9.
5. `dailyAt` is the platform zone (APP_TIMEZONE, default Africa/Casablanca), once per local day from HH:MM, with
   catch-up later the same day. `hourly` is once per UTC hour. `done: false` keeps the period due (the job resumes at
   the next tick); a job that throws is retried at the next tick, 3 times per period at most. No 5-minute window may
   hold more than 2 slot-6 daily jobs (`dailyCollisions`, checked by `test:jobs` on the registry).
6. Per-store `job_runs` rows: a job that iterates with `forEachStore` gets one row per store that did work without
   writing anything. The step callback may now return a number (the rows it handled), typed
   `Promise<void | number>`; a step returning nothing still compiles. Visits picked by a `where` predicate always
   count as work.
7. `JobResult` gained two optional fields: `processed` (`job_runs.processed`) and `errorCode` (the run finished but a
   unit failed: the row is ok = false with that code, the summary kept). `TickReport` gained `notStarted`,
   `leaseLost`, and `slot` / `budgetMs` / `noYield` per job. The frozen fields are unchanged.
8. **P5:** the legacy Sheets shim runs `syncStores` as `sheets.push` (summary `legacy: true`) until a job named
   `sheets.push` is registered. Registering yours switches it off; register `sheets.pull` in the same merge, since the
   shim did push and the due pull together.

## Why

The brief of P8 step 3 fixes the rule "a slot's budget is the guarantee, a job's `budgetMs` is its cap inside the
slot". P1's and P7's plans read §H.2 as "declare the guaranteed part and the runner adds the leftover"; under the cap
rule that would leave the leftover unused, so the numbers above say what to declare.

## Effect on others

None on frozen contracts. Tracks that register jobs choose `budgetMs` by these rules; `test:jobs` (in `npm test` from
the P8 merge) checks them at every later merge.
