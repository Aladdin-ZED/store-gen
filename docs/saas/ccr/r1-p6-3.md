## What

### 1. DES-§4.7 consistency test: the look decisions DES-§4.5.2 and H26 make are not differences

DES-§4.7 asks that `buildTheme(compileGenome(genomeOf(doc)).theme)` emit "exactly the attributes `buildTheme(doc.theme)`
emits today, ignoring only the new `[data-look]`-scoped attributes". Two rules of the same spec forbid that for some
archetype defaults, because the compiled theme carries a `look`:

- DES-§4.5.2: with a look, `data-card` is the card of `look.depth` and `glass` is never the page's card tier (it is a
  per-variant choice inside the G16 budget). An archetype whose default card is `glass` reads back as G16 = 2 and the
  tonal depth, so its compiled theme says `data-card="soft"`.
- H26 (K5): `surfaces = "panels"` only with a flat or hairline card tier. An archetype default of `panels` with a tonal
  or raised depth compiles to `flush`.

Replace the consistency sentence with:

> … emits exactly the attributes `buildTheme(doc.theme)` emits today, ignoring the new `[data-look]`-scoped attributes
> and the three look decisions of DES-§4.5.2 and H26: `data-card` `glass` → the card of the depth, `data-surfaces`
> `panels` → `flush` when the depth is not flat or hairline, and `data-radius` `none` → `subtle` (the ladder's nearest
> legacy kind). An explicit builder override still wins (DS4) and is never one of these.

Measured on the baseline fixtures (`test:design`, group `genome-derive`): 42 docs in the fast run, every other
attribute equal; the exceptions seen are `data-card glass → soft` ×14 and `data-surfaces panels → flush` ×16.

### 2. DES-§25.1 GEN-12: what the sampler alone guarantees

"two stores with identical inputs ≥ 0.25 (GEN-12)" cannot hold for every direction pair from the sampler alone:
direction 1 is the best-fit archetype for both stores (identical inputs, identical ranking), so two stores can draw the
same grammar, strategy, pairing and treatment by chance, and the repulsion step never reads another store's vector
(DS7, D64). The cross-store guarantee is the novelty service's platform band (`too_close` re-sampled in the wizard,
hard at publish). Replace with:

> two stores with identical inputs (brand seeds differ, DS5; each run repelled): mean distance ≥ 0.25 and ≥ 85 % of
> the direction pairs ≥ 0.25 from the sampler alone (GEN-12); the platform band (DES-§7.4, DES-§7.8) catches the rest

Measured: 24 direction pairs (fast) at mean 0.451, one under 0.25 (0.212); 120 pairs (`--full`) at mean 0.442, two
under 0.25 (the lowest 0.078, a near-clone that the platform band's clone flag refuses at publish).

### 3. DES-§4.1 `GenomeCompact`: the layout and the 1,200 B budget

`packGenome` (`src/lib/design/genome.ts`) uses, besides the short keys and the 3-decimal rounding of the spec:

- the sections list `x[i] = [id or "" when id = type, code, G19, arrange]`, the code being the append-only
  `type:variant` token of `tokens-table.ts` (with `.k` for the G29 index); G24.seq = 1 when it is the in-flow order of
  `x`; G33 uses and G34 CTAs as section indices; reveal kinds and openers as index digits; a dropped hint's reason as
  its index; the `why` chip keys without their `design.why.` namespace; G8, G11, G13, G31 and G16 left out at their
  usual values;
- **the budget is kept by cutting only the dropped-hint log**, from its end, when a stored genome would exceed
  1,200 B (a Director brief can drop many values; the render, the distance view and the novelty service never read the
  log, and the sampled genome the wizard logs keeps every entry). Measured: sampled genomes carrying a fixture's 19
  sections, three assets at three uses each and R3 arrangements on five sections pack to 1,087-1,121 B uncut; the
  stress genome (the same with a title structure on every in-flow section, six why keys and twelve dropped hints)
  stays ≤ 1,200 B only through that cut. Before this layout the first three sampled genomes were 1,223-1,246 B.

Add to the `GenomeCompact` comment: "over 1,200 B only `hints.dropped` is shortened (its last entries first)".
