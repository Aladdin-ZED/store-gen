The ids are the ones P6's catalogue already names (`PENDING_FONT_IDS`, `src/lib/theme/archetypes.ts`). Until a face is

manifest grows: the rows enter the catalogue, `theme:check` measures their ink-density ratio (F15) and the full F11
roster rule replaces the fallback rule once Cairo, Kufam and Vazirmatn ship.

### 2. The Arabic metrics step (DES-§10.5 step 1)

`scripts/sync-fonts.mjs`, for every face with an `arabic` subset: reconstruct the WOFF2-transformed `glyf` / `loca`
(W3C WOFF2 §5.1-5.3, + §5.4 `hmtx`), then measure on Arabic glyphs and write
`metrics.ar = { bodyBand, toothHeight, loopHeight, markAbove, markBelow, alefHeight, advanceAvg, measured: true }`.
`FontMetricsData` gains `ar?: { bodyBand: number; toothHeight: number; loopHeight: number; markAbove: number;
markBelow: number; alefHeight: number; advanceAvg: number; measured: true }`.

- `advanceAvg` reads P6's word list **`scripts/sync-fonts/ar-sample.json`** (committed in this step): the 200 most common
  Darija and MSA words of Moroccan COD pages, in rank order, weighted 1/rank.
- The self-check of CRIT2-7: for the faces that ship both formats, the reconstructed boxes equal the `.woff` boxes.
  P6's **`scripts/sync-fonts/ar-glyph-fixture.json`** (committed) holds Tajawal 400's alef and beh boxes and
  `alefHeight` = 645, measured once from the untransformed `tajawal-arabic-400-normal.woff`; `theme:check` compares the
  manifest's `metrics.ar.alefHeight` with it as soon as `measured: true` appears.

P6's `src/lib/design/typography.ts` already reads `FONT_DATA[id].metrics.ar` when `measured: true` and falls back to
its hand-tuned `AR_METRICS_FALLBACK` (`measured: false`) otherwise; `theme:check` lists the faces as
`metrics_missing` (warn) until the step lands.

### 3. The shared WOFF2 module (rev 5, CRIT2-7)

`scripts/fonts/woff2-sfnt.mjs` is the maintainer's. P6 needed outlines in step 1 for F15 and wrote a reader inside its
own files, **`scripts/qa/design-font.ts`** (WOFF2 with the §5.1-5.3 `glyf` reconstruction and the transformed `hmtx`,
WOFF 1.0, TrueType; `cmap` 4 / 12; `fvar` / `avar` / `gvar` with IUP; Presentation Forms-B and `GSUB` single and
ligature substitutions for the Arabic forms). It is offered as the starting point of the shared module: its
`decodeWoff2Glyf` (the triplet decoding, the 255UInt16 stream reads, composite records) passes the CRIT2-7 self-check
today, every one of Tajawal's 187 glyph boxes equals the `.woff` header boxes. When `woff2-sfnt.mjs` lands, P6 switches
`design-font.ts` to import it (no second implementation, DES-§10.5).

## Why

DG-08 (DES-§1.4): F13 and the typography engine need Arabic-measured metrics, R1 exit requires
`metrics.ar.measured = true` for every Arabic display face, and the roster's variety needs the seven faces (UX-8,
UX-9). Agents cannot run `npm install` (P6-10).

## Effect on others

- `fonts.generated.ts` grows by seven faces and an optional `ar` block; `KnownFontId` widens, which every consumer
  accepts. Font files are self-hosted like the 27; `budget:F5` (≤ 2 families, ≤ 4 files per page) is unchanged.
- A document without `look` is unaffected (its fonts are in its ThemeSpec). The legacy no-look golden
  (`src/lib/design/calibration/legacy-theme.json`) does not depend on the manifest's size.
- When the faces ship, P6 runs `npx tsx scripts/qa/design-ink.ts --tune` on the 22 pending rows; any row outside
  0.85-1.15 is re-tuned in `src/lib/theme/archetypes.ts` (P6's file), never shifted at compile (F15).
