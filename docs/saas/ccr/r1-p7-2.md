## Why

- AI-§D.2 rule 5 (rev 3, QUAL-17): number words count as numbers, and "the library's hand-written lines are scanned by
  `test:ai` with the same function (a library line that fails is a bug in the library, fixed, not waived)". "Trois
  gestes", "en une heure", "deux à trois fois par semaine", "ثلاث خطوات", "دقيقة واحدة", "thirty seconds" state
  figures no merchant gave. The steps titles are true by construction today (the section has three steps) but tie the
  copy to a count the builder lets the owner change.
- AI-§D.2 rule 9 and DES-§9.5 (DG-07): no em or en dash in the library (its own acceptance: the grep for an em or en dash counts 0 lines).
- The Darija guide (AI-§F.2, QUAL-30) finds no misspelt Darija word in `ar.ts` (vowel marks on MSA words are not a
  spelling and are not judged).

Until the lines change, `src/lib/ai/library-scan.ts` lists the 74 keys in `PENDING_LIBRARY_CCR` (printed by `test:ai`;
`test:ai --strict`, the R1 integration's run, fails while the list is not empty). A key whose line no longer fails is
reported as stale, so the list shrinks with the fix.

Out of the scan by design: the offer names (`common.offers.*`, `common.bundles.*`: "1 + 1", "Les trois réunis" name
the structure of the very offer they label, docs/OFFERS.md §H.1).

  are structural). The DG-07 structure tags of the changed steps titles move from `number-led` / `noun-phrase` to
  `noun-phrase`; the "≥ 3 structures per family × locale for hero titles" rule is unaffected (no hero title changes).
- P7: delete the applied keys from `PENDING_LIBRARY_CCR` (`test:ai` reports them stale).
- No frozen contract changes.
