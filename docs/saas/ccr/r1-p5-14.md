# r1-p5-14: the tenancy harness follows the Sheets connect flow from the routes to the actions

- Status: open (the maintainer applies it in `scripts/qa/tenancy-check.ts` when P5's step 10 lands; P5 keeps the routes until then)
- Owner of the change: maintainer (`scripts/qa/tenancy-check.ts`, §K.3)
- Asked by: P5 INTEGRATIONS, step 8 (`src/app/app/[store]/integrations/sheets/actions.ts`, `src/app/api/app/[store]/sheets/**`)

## What

Step 8 added the server actions of OPE-§M.3 (`startSheetConnectAction`, `verifySheetConnectAction`,
`disconnectSheetAction`, `setSheetsEnabledAction`, `setSheetsPullAction`, `syncSheetNowAction`, `repairSheetAction`,
`requeueSheetRowAction`), with their attack cases in `scripts/qa/tenancy-cases/integrations.ts` (`actions`).

The R1.0 routes `POST|PATCH|DELETE /api/app/[store]/sheets/connect`, `POST …/verify` and `POST …/test` still have one
caller, the R1.0 wizard (`src/components/admin/settings/sheets-wizard.tsx`), which P5's step 10 replaces with the new
Sheets screen on the actions. Step 10 then deletes those three routes (they are P5's). The harness names them in:

- the route catalogue bodies: `"POST /api/app/[store]/sheets/connect"`, `"PATCH /api/app/[store]/sheets/connect"`
  (two places, A's and B's bodies);
- the special case "B pastes A's spreadsheet / verifies a proof for it" (`callRoute(… "/api/app/[store]/sheets/connect" …)`,
  `… "/sheets/verify" …`);
- the §B.7 step-up case (`connectRoute`, `verifyRoute`: `403 step_up`).

The diff at that merge: drop the two body entries; point the special case at the actions
(`actionFn({ file: "src/app/app/[store]/integrations/sheets/actions.ts", name: "startSheetConnectAction" })(link)` →
`refusal(r) === "invalid"` with `message === "int.sheets.refused"`, the same for `verifySheetConnectAction()`); and point
the step-up case at the same two actions (`refusal(r) === "step_up"` without a fresh step-up, `impersonation_read_only`
under impersonation).

Kept for good: `GET …/status` (a client poll) and `POST …/sync` (the R1.0 dashboard widget and the orders list still
fetch it through `src/components/admin/lib/sheets-client.ts`). Step 8 changed `…/sync` to the OPE-§M.2 guard
(`integrations.manage`, feature `sheets`; it was `orders.write`) and to the same run as `syncSheetNowAction`
(`syncSheetNow`, bucket `s:<sid>:sheets:sync:<uid>` 12 / 10 min shared).

## Why

OPE-§M.2 / the step brief: keep only the routes a client fetch still needs; delete the ones whose callers moved to
the actions. The harness is maintainer-owned, so P5 cannot move its references itself.

## Meanwhile

Nothing breaks: the routes stay until step 10, and the harness keeps testing them.
