# r1-p8-7: call `syncAssetRefs` / `assetUsage` from save, publish, restore and the brand setting

- **R1 P8 merge (maintainer, 2026-11-06, speed mode):** deferred to the R1 integration (speed mode): assetUsage keeps its exact doc scan meanwhile and refs.reconcile heals the index nightly; the wiring touches pages.server.ts, publish.server.ts and settings, which later tracks also change.
- Status: **open, diff below** (P8 step 6 wrote `src/lib/storage/asset-refs.ts`). P0b triage: defer → P8 merge.
- Filed by: P8 · Owner of the files: maintainer · Plan item: P8-7
- Files: `src/lib/editor/pages.server.ts`, `src/lib/admin-data/pages.ts`, `src/lib/render/publish.server.ts`,
  `src/lib/settings/index.ts`, `src/lib/admin-data/assets.ts`

## What

Call P8's `syncAssetRefs(scope, ownerKind, ownerId, ids, { live })` (`src/lib/storage/asset-refs.ts`; §G.5 names it
`src/lib/media/refs.ts`, see r1-p8-report.md):

- on draft save, version restore and page creation (owner `page`, live false);
- on publish, the pixels-only publish included (owner `page_version`, live true; the version it replaced loses its
  rows) and on unpublish (the live version's rows become live false);
- on writes of the `brand` setting (owner `brand`, live true).

Replace the LIKE scans of `src/lib/admin-data/assets.ts:78-107` with `assetUsageByPage(scope, id)`.

A failed sync never fails the save or the publish: it is logged, and the nightly `refs.reconcile` (P8 step 7) heals
the index.

## Why

§G.5 (OS-07): refs run on every save, publish, restore and settings write, and the LIKE scans go. Until this lands,
`assetUsage` and `livePagesUsing` also scan the store's drafts, live versions and brand logo exactly (`collectAssetIds`
over the parsed JSON, ≤ `pages_max` documents, no LIKE); P8 step 12 removes that scan once this CCR is applied.
