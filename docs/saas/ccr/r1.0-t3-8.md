# r1.0-t3-8: the console host (`ADMIN_HOST = console.<domain>`) must not run through the store Worker

- Status: applied in fa880e4 (T3 merge): STORE-SAAS §C.5 (the console route exclusion paragraph), §C.10 steps 1 and 3
  (V12 + `x-sg-mode`), RESEARCH §5.2 (the owner step after the Worker route), HANDOFF §6 (domain-move checklist).
- Filed by: T3 · Owner of the file: maintainer
- Files: `docs/STORE-SAAS.md` (§C.5, §C.10 step 1), `docs/saas/RESEARCH.md` §5.2 (owner runbook), `docs/HANDOFF.md` (domain-move checklist)

## What

1. §C.5, after "Route: `*.<domain>/*` only (the apex never runs the Worker; V3 confirms the pattern)", add:

   > **The console host is not a store host.** Add a second Cloudflare route `console.<domain>/*` with Worker
   > **None** (a route exclusion), and serve `console.<domain>` from Hostinger as its own hostname (the probe V2
   > decides how: a domain alias on the Node site, or a proxied record whose origin Host is `console.<domain>`).
   > Through `edge/worker.js` the console would be unusable: the Worker answers `/admin/**` and `/api/admin/**`
   > with its own 404, deletes `Cookie` (the `__Host-cs` / `__Host-csg` session and gate cookies) and strips every
   > `Set-Cookie`, and Next's Server Actions check would compare `Origin: https://console.<domain>` with the apex
   > `Host` of the subrequest.

2. §C.10 step 1, after "`ADMIN_HOST=console.<domain>` (D44 rev 3)", add: "(reached directly, never through the
   Worker: the route exclusion of §C.5)". Step 3, V12: add "and `curl -sI https://console.<domain>/admin` has no
   `x-sg-mode` header".

3. RESEARCH §5.2 and the HANDOFF domain-move checklist: the route exclusion as an owner step, right after "Worker +
   secret + route".

No code changes: `classifyHost` already gives `ADMIN_HOST` the class `console` on either path, and the proxy's
console rule (`src/proxy.ts` `onConsole`) needs the request's cookies, which only a direct request keeps.

## Why

§C.2 lists `ADMIN_HOST` as "either" (edge or direct), and domains.md line 202 says "console.maindomain via the
Worker". But the Worker of §C.5, which `edge/worker.js` implements literally, is a store-host Worker: its path
refusals, its Cookie deletion (§C.9: "store hosts never use cookies") and its `Set-Cookie` stripping are the
store-host controls. Running the console through it would break sign-in at the domain move (D44), the day the
console must leave the shared origin. Moving the console into the Worker would mean a host-specific branch that
forwards cookies, and that weakens the one rule the Worker exists to enforce.

## Until applied

Nothing breaks in R1.0. The console stays on the apex at `/admin/**` until the domain move. The risk is the owner
following §C.10 as written on the day of the move.
