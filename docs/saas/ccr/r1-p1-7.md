## 1. `SignupSignals.inputs.via` gains `"console"` (maintainer, `src/lib/db/schema.ts`)

### Why

PLA-§6.2: a store made at `/admin/stores/new` gets an approved `signup_reviews` row (`reason_code = "console_created"`).
Its `signals.inputs.via` has no honest value today (`signup | app_new | invite_code | waitlist_invite`).
`src/lib/platform/store-create.ts` writes `"console"` through a cast meanwhile.

### What

```diff
-    via: "signup" | "app_new" | "invite_code" | "waitlist_invite";
+    via: "signup" | "app_new" | "invite_code" | "waitlist_invite" | "console";
```

Then the cast in `consoleSignals()` goes (one line).

## 2. P4's console reads (P4, `src/lib/orders/profile.ts`)

1. **A 90-day `orderKpis` window.** PLA-§7.3's KPI row has a 7 / 30 / 90-day range; `orderKpis` measures `today`,
   `7d` and `28d`. Meanwhile the console's 30-day range reads the 28-day window and says so, and the 90-day range shows
   "unavailable" for the order tiles (the visitors still come from `stats_daily`). Ask: `window: "today" | "7d" | "28d" |
   "90d"` (additive).
2. **`listOrdersForConsole` filters `riskMin` and `pageId`.** PLA-§7.4's filters "risk ≥ 70" and "page" are applied to
   the page P4 returns, so a filtered page may hold fewer than 50 rows (the cursor stays P4's). Ask: `q.riskMin?: number;
   q.pageId?: string` (additive).
3. **The reveal's note.** PLA-§7.4: the reason `other` needs a note, and `recordPlatformReveal(ctx, scope, orderId,
   reason)` is the single audit writer, so the note is checked and then dropped. Ask: `reason: string, note?: string`
   (≤ 300, into the audit meta) (additive).
4. **Agents online.** `OpsPulse` has no `agentsOnline` (PLA-§7.3 strip 5 names it); the console reads
   `queueCounts(scope).liveAgents`. If P4 means another number, P4 says which.

## 3. T1's `listPlatformAudit` filters (T1, `src/lib/audit.ts`)

PLA-§7.7 filters the store's Activity by staff member and date. `listPlatformAudit` takes `storeId`, `actorKind`,
`area` and `beforeId` only, so `src/lib/platform/profile/activity.ts` applies the staff member and the dates to the
page it returns. Ask (additive, like P0b's CCR P2-12 for `listStoreAudit`): `userId?: string; from?: Date; to?: Date`.

## 4. Who audits a staff export (P8, `src/lib/exports-store/index.ts`)

PLA-§7.14: a console export audits `store.export_requested` with `actor_kind = 'platform'` and notifies
`store.export_by_platform`. `src/lib/platform/exports.ts` writes both after `requestStoreExport(platformScope, {
requestedAs: "platform", requestedBy })`. P8's function must not write a second `store.export_requested` row for
`requestedAs: "platform"` (it does for the owner's own request). Same for `store.export_downloaded`: the console's
download route writes it for its staff links (`openExportPart`).
