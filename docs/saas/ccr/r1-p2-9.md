## 1. `POST /api/app/<h>/media`: `used` and `limit` in the 413 `quota` body

File: `src/app/api/app/[store]/media/route.ts` (core §K.1, T4; not P2's).

Why: PLA-§22.2 says a 413 quota answer on the welcome photos step (the pending store's cap, D69) opens the limit
sheet. The frozen `LimitSheetProps` quota variant needs `used` and `limit`, and the route answers only
`{ ok: false, error: "quota", message }` today. The `QuotaError` it catches already holds both numbers.

Diff:

```diff
-    if (err instanceof QuotaError) return json({ ok: false, error: "quota", message: QUOTA_MESSAGES[quotaKind(err)] }, 413);
+    if (err instanceof QuotaError) return json({ ok: false, error: "quota", message: QUOTA_MESSAGES[quotaKind(err)], metric: err.metric.metric, used: err.used, limit: err.limit }, 413);
```

Meanwhile: `src/app/app/[store]/welcome/_components/photo-upload.ts` reads `used` / `limit` from the body when they are
numbers, else the welcome page's own media gauge (`quotaGauges` under the pending cap of `reviewFacts`), so the sheet
opens either way. Nothing else changes when the CCR lands.

File: `src/components/ui/switch.tsx` (core §K.1, T4; not P2's).

Why: the checked thumb moves with `data-checked:translate-x-[calc(100%-2px)]`, a physical direction. Under `dir="rtl"`
(every ar screen) the unchecked thumb sits on the right and the checked one moves further right, out of the track
(seen on the welcome contact step at 390 px in ar).

```diff
-group-data-[size=default]/switch:data-checked:translate-x-[calc(100%-2px)]
+group-data-[size=default]/switch:data-checked:translate-x-[calc(100%-2px)] rtl:group-data-[size=default]/switch:data-checked:-translate-x-[calc(100%-2px)]

+group-data-[size=sm]/switch:data-checked:translate-x-[calc(100%-2px)] rtl:group-data-[size=sm]/switch:data-checked:-translate-x-[calc(100%-2px)]
```

Meanwhile: the welcome contact step wraps its switch in `dir="ltr"` (`welcome-flow.tsx`). Every other switch on an ar
screen uses the same classes (e.g. P2's `require-2fa.tsx`, not shot in ar yet), so this one change covers them all.
