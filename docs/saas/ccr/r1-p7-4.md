## Why

1. P6's `src/lib/ai/copy-library/selftest.ts` reads `c.hero.kicker` as a string, and DES-§5.8 keeps the hero's
   kicker; making it optional would break P6's typecheck for no behaviour (the guard never drops the hero's kicker).
2. The brief of P7 step 2: existing callers (`kit.ts`, `wizard.tsx:614`, `src/lib/generator/selftest.ts`) "compile and
   behave as today when the new fields are absent". P6's generator selftest asserts that "Trois gouttes" and "Une
   minute suffit." are accepted, which rule 5's number words refuse.
3. Without it every French and English line with an article ("un geste simple", "one piece of each colour") would fail
   the number rule; the first library scan read dozens of such article phrases as figures.
4. The shapes P7 built (types in `src/lib/ai/schema/tasks/*.ts`, narrowed in step 1, P0b P0-3).
5. `punctuate` is not exported by the P0 stub (`src/lib/design/cliche.ts`).
