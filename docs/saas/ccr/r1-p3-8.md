## Why

LAN-§10.1 and §10.6 bullet 3 ("an EU-market page has no `endpoints.lead`; an MA page has it"); the stub's expectation
was marked as temporary in the test's own comment.

## Effect

an EU store's live and test documents lose `endpoints.lead`, as the spec asks. `test:leads` already proves both sides
(`storeEndpoints` for MA and FR, and `upsertLead` dropping an EU beacon).
