## 1. `NicheShares` carries no archetype share (N5, "H33 on G1 shares")

DES-§4.3 and DES-§5.7 N5 say direction 1 is "the best fit among the de-concentrated set" and that H33 applies to G1
shares. `NicheShares` (`src/lib/design/novelty.server.ts`, its key set frozen by
`src/lib/contracts/r1-p0.ts:834`) has no G1 entry, so the sampler cannot read the niche's archetype shares.

Without them, the N5 sweep sits at 55-70 % for the top archetype per category (owners pick direction 1 on 55 % of
pages, and the ranking is stable within a category). With the shares fed back, it holds ≤ 45 %.

**Today (P6 code, no shared file touched):** `directionArchetype` (solve.ts) and `pickArchetypes` /
`generateDirections` (directions.ts) take an optional `vibeShares: { n, shares }` (k ≥ 10, rounded to 10 %), with the
step factor at the cap `CONCENTRATION_CAPS.vibe = 0.35` (× 0.5 from 0.28, × 0.25 from 0.35, on the best-fit slot and
on the draws). The N5 test feeds the chosen pages back as shares.

**Ask (maintainer):** widen the frozen key set by one key:

```diff

+    Equals<keyof NicheShares, "n" | "grammar" | "strategy" | "heroFamily" | "heroArrangement" | "displayFamily" | "arDisplay" | "arDisplayClass" | "motion" | "ghost" | "seq4" | "tells" | "vibe">
```

P6 then adds `vibe: Record<string, number>` to `NicheShares` (step 9, the novelty service computes it like the other
shares), and `generateDirections` reads `input.shares.vibe` when `vibeShares` is absent. Until then the wizard passes
nothing and the draw stays neutral, which is today's behaviour.

## 2. The expansion's I2 / I8 repairs and the fallback (DES-§5.1, DES-§5.5 step 5)

The measured heights of step 3 are much larger than the DES-§5.3 type constants for some variants:
`benefits:stack` is 5.15 screens, `benefits:bento` 3.4 and `benefits:zigzag` 2.95, against "benefits 1.3". With them,
a draw-and-retry expansion fell back on 15 % of 2,000 expansions, and today's fixed fallback orders broke I8
(up to 15,816 px) because they always carried `benefits`.

**What the code does (`src/lib/design/choreography.ts`):**

- **I2 decides the Act1 length** (the DES-§5.1 "≤ 2 blocks, I2 decides" row taken literally). A block that pushes the
  form past 2,532 px leaves Act1, and may still come back in Act2. If the form is still too low, the strip leaves.
- **I8 decides the Act2 length.** While the page estimates over 12,000 px, the heaviest Act2 block leaves. The block
  the page closes on is kept.
- **Close → ε** needs an Act2 block that already asks (`offer:showcase`, `guarantee:split`). That block is pinned last,
  and the other blocks are ordered before it. Before this, a draw could order the asking block away from the end.
- **The fallback** keeps the three short orders, and repairs each one against the page's own variants: a block the
  inputs cannot fill leaves; the heaviest optional block leaves while I2 or I8 fails; a light block of a missing
  layout family is added while the page has fewer than 8 sections or fewer than 4 families; a moment-capable block
  moves up under the form for I4.

- **Planner margins**: the expansion targets 2,482 px for the form top and 11,700 px for the page, a little inside
  I2 and I8. The document's estimate reads the final spacing pass, which the expansion only approximates (±2 %).

Result: 0 fallbacks over 2,000 expansions (1,722 distinct sequences) and over 10,000 (7,004 distinct). I1-I8 and H35
hold on 100 % of expansions, the form starts at ≤ 2,481 px, and pages are ≤ 11,700 px.

**Ask (lead):** add the two "decides" sentences and the pinned ε close to DES-§5.5 step 1. Change DES-§5.5 step 5 to
say "repaired against the page's variants". Re-read the DES-§5.1 Act1 length row with the measured heights: the
compact benefits variant that row names is `hanging` (0.95), not `stack`.

## 3. The colour moment of I4 on a planned page

`assignBackgrounds` reads the "top half" of a planned page on the estimated mobile height (`finishPlan` with the
page's density genes), not on the section count. If no moment type sits in that half, the moment goes on any content
block there, and a strong strip, block or final cta right next to it steps back to a quiet ground: I4 outranks the
close's moment. On a planned calm page the strips stay quiet, so its two moments go to content. Pages without a look
keep today's rule unchanged. **Ask (lead):** one sentence in DES-§5.5 step 4 ("the half is the estimated height's").

## 4. The hero choreography row (DES-§4.12) is a planner output

`heroChoreography(grammar, family, G23)` (`src/lib/design/reveals.ts`) returns the row of the grammar a family serves
(`index` → catalogue, `contact` → gallery-led, `statement` by its structure), the slots with their offsets and reveal
kinds, and the stagger. The stagger is held so the row totals ≤ 1,400 ms. `test:design` (group `rhythm`) checks two
things: two genomes with one G1 and different G2 give different rows, and every row is ≤ 1,400 ms. The compile
emission of `--lp-reveal-i` per slot comes with the renderer work. **Ask:** none; for information.
