## What

LAN-§23.7's "Fix" column sends a Store Health issue to a builder tab or section ("Offers tab", "Product tab", "SEO
tab", "Pixels tab" = the SEO & tracking tab, "Form tab", "the section"). P3's rules (src/lib/health/rules/landing.ts)
now link `appHref(handle, "/pages/<id>/edit?tab=<LeftTab>")` or `…/edit?section=<sectionId>`. The builder has no deep
link yet; this CCR adds one, read once when the builder opens.

## Why

- LAN-§23.7 names a tab or a section for ten of the seventeen rules; core §I.7 asks each issue's fix to land where the
  owner can act. Without the deep link the owner lands on the builder's default tab and has to find the field.
- The same link serves the later P3 screens (Pages list vitals, the Leads inbox's page column) and P1's console
  preview of a store's issues, which read `issue.fix.href`.

## Effect

- No change without the query. The values are validated (a tab of the page's kind, a section of its document,
  `[A-Za-z0-9_-]{1,40}`), never rendered, never stored.
- `test:honesty` (P3) pins every rule's fix link: `…/edit?tab=offers` for `countdown_ended`, `stock_zero`,
  `stock_stale`, `fee_mismatch`; `?tab=product` for `compare_not_higher`, `no_alt`; `?tab=seo` for `no_seo`,
  `no_pixel`; `?tab=form` for `form_hidden`; `?section=<first unattested proof section>` for `proof_unattested`.
