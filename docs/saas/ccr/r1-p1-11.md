## 1. The Orders tab's `source` column (P4, `ConsoleOrderRow`)

### Why

PLA-§7.4 lists the Orders tab's columns as "id, created (store time), status / `confirm_state`, total, masked customer,
page, risk score chip (≥ 70 crimson), source", and says manual phone / WhatsApp orders "show their `source` chip like any
other". The frozen `ConsoleOrderRow` (`src/lib/orders/profile.ts`, OPE-§27.1) has no `source`, so the console cannot
show it without reading `orders` itself (which PLA Q.11 forbids: the console reads orders through P4 only).

### What

Add `source` (the existing `orders.source` enum code: `live` / `export` / `php` / `manual` / `api` / `storefront` /
`whatsapp`, never a free text) to `ConsoleOrderRow` and therefore to `ConsoleOrderDetail`, and to the frozen copy in
`src/lib/contracts/r1-p0.ts`. P1 then adds the column (`console.order_source.<code>`) to
`src/components/console/profile/orders-tab.tsx`. Until then the table has the other seven columns and the page
column says "Store checkout" for page-less orders, as specified.

## 2. The Overview KPI row's confirmed sales and daily confirmed series (P4, `orderKpis`)

### Why

PLA-§7.3 asks the KPI row for "confirmed sales" and a chart of "daily orders placed vs confirmed (two series, neutral and
jade)". `OrderKpis` carries counts only (`placed`, `confirmed`, `delivered`, the rates) and no per-day series; and its
windows are `today` / `7d` / `28d`, so the 30- and 90-day ranges have no P4 window (the 30-day range reads 28 days
and says so; the 90-day range says "The call-centre figures cover 7 and 28 days only", already noted in CCR r1-p1-7
item 2).

### What

Additive fields on `OrderKpis` (no rename): `confirmedCents: number` (confirmed orders' totals in the store currency,
test orders excluded) and `days: { day: string; placed: number; confirmed: number }[]` (the window's days in the store
time zone). With them, `src/components/console/profile/kpi-row.tsx` fills the "Confirmed sales" tile (today "Not
available" with the reason) and draws the jade series (today its legend says "Confirmed per day: not available yet").

## 3. `KeysetPager` keeps only its own parameter (P2, `keyset-pager.tsx`)

### Why

The P0b stub builds `?cursor=<c>` from scratch, which drops the profile's filters (`state`, `risky`, `view`, `actor`, …)
when paging. The console's lists keep filters and cursor together in the URL (PLA-§4.5).

### What

In P2's body, build the link from the current search parameters with only `param` replaced. Until the merge, the
profile pages with its own `src/components/console/profile/older-link.tsx` (the same rule), which the verify step can
swap for `KeysetPager` once the body keeps the other parameters.

## 4. The header's primitives at their profile sizes (P2)

`Monogram` at 48 px, `HealthRing` at 40 px with `surface="console"`, `ModeBadge` and `StatusPill size="md"` render the
P0b stubs today; `src/components/console/shell/console.module.css` lays them out with `:not([class])` rules (added in
this step), which stop matching once P2's bodies carry classes. Nothing to change in the props.
